About

I'm a Computer Science graduate (B.Tech, IKGPTU Kapurthala, 2021–2025) and a full-stack developer based in New Delhi. I like working on products that people actually depend on every day — not demo apps.

At work I develop Pehlwan, a gym-management platform. The app handles the unglamorous but important stuff: owners and trainers log in with a WhatsApp OTP, members check their plans and attendance, and gym owners manage staff, inventory and subscriptions across multiple branches. My part of it is the React Native app and the Express + MongoDB API behind it. It's a commercial product, so the code stays private — but it's where most of my recent engineering has happened.

Before that I did a MERN stack internship at QSpiders, where I built a complete learning management system from scratch — course publishing, payments, progress tracking and an admin dashboard. These days I'm also learning how to build AI features into products like these.
